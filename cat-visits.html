Cat Visits

Drop-in visits are a great fit for cats who need care, attention, feeding, potty breaks, and a dependable check-in while you’re away. Pawsitive Pet Care provides thoughtful in-home visits that help keep your cat comfortable and your routine on track.

We offer a free consultation to meet you and your cat before getting started, so we can learn your routine and make sure the service is the right fit.

Visit Client Portal Contact Us

More service details, scheduling, and client information are available in the Client Portal.

Pricing

  • 20 minute cat visit: $15
  • 30 minute cat visit: $25
  • 45 minute cat visit: $30

What’s Included

  • Feeding and fresh water
  • Litter check as needed
  • Playtime, companionship, and attention
  • A dependable visit that helps maintain routine

Good Fit For

  • Cats who need a quick midday check-in
  • Feeding visits while you're at work or away
  • Cats who benefit from consistency and attention
  • Families who want dependable in-home care

Getting Started

Start with a free consultation so we can meet you and your cats, understand the routine, and answer any questions before services begin.

After that, scheduling and service details are available through the Client Portal.
