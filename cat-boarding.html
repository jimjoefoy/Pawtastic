Cat Care

Pawsitive Pet Care offers attentive cat visits and in-home cat boarding with a calm, thoughtful approach. Whether your cat needs a quick visit, extra time and attention, or a safe place to stay while you’re away, care is tailored to your cat’s personality and routine.

We offer a free consultation to learn more about your cat’s needs and help you decide which service is the best fit.

Visit Client Portal Contact Us

More service details, scheduling, and client information are available in the Client Portal.

Cat Visit Pricing

  • 20 minute cat visit: $15
  • 30 minute cat visit: $20
  • 45 minute cat visit: $30

Boarding Rates

  • Normal rate: $35 per night for 1 cat
  • Normal rate: $45 per night for 2 cats from the same household
  • Holiday rate: $45 per night for 1 cat
  • Holiday rate: $55 per night for 2 cats from the same household

Holiday pricing applies during peak times such as the weeks of New Year’s, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas.

In-Home Cat Boarding

Cat boarding is offered in my home, where your feline companion will have their own private, spacious, cage-free, climate-controlled room in a safe, clean, and comfortable environment.

The room is approximately 14.5 feet by 10.5 feet and includes two large windows that provide sunlight, entertainment, and reduced stress.

It is fully equipped with scratching posts, cushioned beds, a cat tree to climb, plenty of fresh water, and litter boxes that are cleaned multiple times each day.

What’s Included

  • Private, cage-free room for your cat
  • Fresh water and daily care and attention
  • Litter boxes cleaned multiple times each day
  • Scratching posts, beds, and climbing space
  • Daily updates with photos
  • No unattended overnight stays

Medication & Special Care

Medications can be administered at no extra charge, including pills, ear drops, and insulin injections for well-controlled diabetes.

Care is tailored to your cat’s routine and comfort level, with attention to both physical needs and stress reduction.

Household & Capacity

Because of the large size of the room, I can accommodate up to 2 cats from the same household.

I never board cats from different homes in the same room.

I also have experience fostering cats in my home since 2021 through the Humane Society of Utah and the Madison Cat Project.

Requirements

  • Documentation of current rabies vaccination is required
  • Documentation of current FVRCP vaccination is required
  • Please provide enough food for your cat’s full stay
  • Special or prescription diets should be sent with your cat

What To Bring

Please bring enough of your cat’s food to last throughout their stay, along with any treats or snacks they normally enjoy.

Bringing familiar items such as a cat bed, blankets, pillows, towels, or favorite toys may help your cat feel more comfortable and settled.

Scheduling & Location

Flexible pick-up and drop-off scheduling is available near MM and Foxfield Road in Oregon, WI.

Cancellation Policy

At least 24 hours notice is required for cancellations.

If a cancellation is made with less than 24 hours notice, a 50% cancellation fee will be charged.

Getting Started

Start with a free consultation so we can learn about your cat’s routine, personality, preferences, medications, and any special care details.

After that, scheduling and client information are available through the Client Portal.
