Cat Boarding

Pawsitive Pet Care offers in-home cat boarding with a calm, safe and comfortable place to stay.

Visit Client Portal Contact Us

More service details and scheduling are available in the Client Portal.

Boarding Rates

  • $35/night (1 cat)
  • $45/night (2 cats, same household)
  • $45/night holiday (1 cat)
  • $55/night holiday (2 cats, same household)

Private In-Home Boarding

Your cat will stay in a private, cage-free, climate-controlled room (~14.5’ x 10.5’) with natural sunlight from two large windows.

The space includes scratching posts, beds, a cat tree, fresh water, and litter boxes cleaned multiple times daily.

  • Medication administration (no extra charge)
  • Daily updates with photos
  • Overnight supervision

Experience & Capacity

Up to 2 cats from the same household are accommodated. Cats are never mixed between households.

Experience includes fostering since 2021 with the Humane Society of Utah and Madison Cat Project.

Requirements

  • Rabies vaccination required
  • FVRCP vaccination required
  • Provide enough food for entire stay

What To Bring

Food, treats, and familiar items like beds, blankets, or toys to help your cat feel comfortable.

Policies

24-hour notice required for cancellations or 50% fee may apply.

Flexible drop-off near MM & Foxfield Rd in Oregon, WI.
