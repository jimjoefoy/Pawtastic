Dog Walks

Reliable walks tailored to your dog’s pace, personality, energy level, and routine. Whether your dog needs a mid-day break, consistent exercise, or a longer outing, Pawsitive Pet Care provides dependable walks with thoughtful care and updates that keep you in the loop.

We offer a free consultation to meet you and your dog before getting started, so everyone feels comfortable and confident from day one.

What’s Included

  • Walks tailored to your dog’s pace and energy level
  • Fresh water and a quick check-in after each visit
  • Attention to weather, safety, and your dog’s comfort
  • Dependable care with thoughtful updates

Good Fit For

  • Dogs who need exercise during the day
  • Busy work schedules and long days away from home
  • Dogs who benefit from routine and consistency
  • Pet parents who want reliable care they can trust
